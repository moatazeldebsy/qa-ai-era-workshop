    Further reading¶

    A short list per topic: the sources worth your time once you've done the lab. Books are marked 📘.

    1–2 · Foundations and test design¶

    • 📘 Lessons Learned in Software Testing, Kaner, Bach & Pettichord
    • 📘 Explore It!, Elisabeth Hendrickson
    • 📘 The Art of Software Testing, Myers, Sandler & Badgett: equivalence partitioning, boundaries, decision tables
    • ISO/IEC 25010 product quality model
    • fast-check documentation: property-based testing in JavaScript

    3 · Unit and component testing¶

    • 📘 Unit Testing: Principles, Practices, and Patterns, Vladimir Khorikov
    • 📘 Test-Driven Development: By Example, Kent Beck
    • Mocks Aren't Stubs, Martin Fowler
    • Stryker mutation testing

    4 · Integration, API and contract testing¶

    • Pact documentation: consumer-driven contracts
    • Contract Test, Martin Fowler
    • OpenAPI specification

    5 · UI, web and end-to-end testing¶

    • Playwright documentation: especially Best Practices and Locators
    • The Practical Test Pyramid, Ham Vocke
    • Eradicating Non-Determinism in Tests, Martin Fowler

    6 · CI/CD and continuous testing¶

    • 📘 Continuous Delivery, Jez Humble & David Farley
    • GitHub Actions: reusing workflows

    7 · Test environments and test data¶

    • Docker Compose documentation
    • Testcontainers

    8 · Performance, load and resilience¶

    • Grafana k6 documentation: thresholds, scenarios, executors
    • 📘 Release It!, Michael Nygard: timeouts, circuit breakers, bulkheads
    • Principles of Chaos Engineering

    9 · Security, accessibility and compatibility¶

    • OWASP Top 10 and the OWASP Cheat Sheet Series
    • OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications
    • WCAG 2.2 at a glance
    • axe-core rules

    10 · Production quality and observability¶

    • Google SRE Book, especially Service Level Objectives and Monitoring Distributed Systems
    • The Site Reliability Workbook: implementing SLOs and alerting on burn rate
    • OpenTelemetry documentation
    • Prometheus: histograms and summaries

    11 · Test management and quality intelligence¶

    • DORA research and the four key metrics
    • 📘 Accelerate, Forsgren, Humble & Kim

    12 · AI in QA engineering¶

    • promptfoo documentation: evals, assertions, red teaming
    • Anthropic: define success criteria and build evaluations
    • NIST AI Risk Management Framework

    13 · QA platform engineering¶

    • 📘 Team Topologies, Matthew Skelton & Manuel Pais
    • CNCF Platforms white paper

    14 · Quality strategy and leadership¶

    • 📘 Agile Testing and More Agile Testing, Lisa Crispin & Janet Gregory
    • 📘 The Fearless Organization, Amy Edmondson: psychological safety
    • Google SRE Book: postmortem culture
