# Code of Conduct

This course is a place to learn, and learning means asking questions that might sound basic, getting things wrong, and sharing unfinished work. We want everyone to feel safe doing that.

## Our standards

Do:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the field or writing in a second language.
- Critique ideas and code, never people.
- Explain, instead of just correcting. Link to the part of the course that helps.
- Give credit when you build on someone else's work.

Don't:

- Harass, insult, or demean anyone, publicly or privately.
- Make sexualised comments, or unwelcome advances.
- Discriminate against anyone for any reason, including age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, experience, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.
- Share anyone's private information without their permission.
- Spam, or advertise unrelated products or services.

## Scope

This applies in all course spaces: issues, pull requests, Discussions, and study groups or events run under the course's name.

## Reporting

If something happens that makes you or someone else uncomfortable, contact the maintainer, [@moatazeldebsy](https://github.com/moatazeldebsy), through a private channel on their GitHub profile. You can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on any comment. Reports are handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, and warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour breaks this code, depending on how serious and how repeated it is.
