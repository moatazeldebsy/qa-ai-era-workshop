# Further reading

A short list per topic: the sources worth your time once you've done the lab. Books are marked 📘.

## 1–2 · Foundations and test design

- 📘 *Lessons Learned in Software Testing*, Kaner, Bach & Pettichord
- 📘 *Explore It!*, Elisabeth Hendrickson
- 📘 *The Art of Software Testing*, Myers, Sandler & Badgett: equivalence partitioning, boundaries, decision tables
- [ISO/IEC 25010 product quality model](https://iso25000.com/index.php/en/iso-25000-standards/iso-25010)
- [fast-check documentation](https://fast-check.dev/): property-based testing in JavaScript

## 3 · Unit and component testing

- 📘 *Unit Testing: Principles, Practices, and Patterns*, Vladimir Khorikov
- 📘 *Test-Driven Development: By Example*, Kent Beck
- [Mocks Aren't Stubs](https://martinfowler.com/articles/mocksArentStubs.html), Martin Fowler
- [Stryker mutation testing](https://stryker-mutator.io/docs/)

## 4 · Integration, API and contract testing

- [Pact documentation](https://docs.pact.io/): consumer-driven contracts
- [Contract Test](https://martinfowler.com/bliki/ContractTest.html), Martin Fowler
- [OpenAPI specification](https://spec.openapis.org/oas/latest.html)

## 5 · UI, web and end-to-end testing

- [Playwright documentation](https://playwright.dev/docs/intro): especially *Best Practices* and *Locators*
- [The Practical Test Pyramid](https://martinfowler.com/articles/practical-test-pyramid.html), Ham Vocke
- [Eradicating Non-Determinism in Tests](https://martinfowler.com/articles/nonDeterminism.html), Martin Fowler

## 6 · CI/CD and continuous testing

- 📘 *Continuous Delivery*, Jez Humble & David Farley
- [GitHub Actions: reusing workflows](https://docs.github.com/en/actions/sharing-automations/reusing-workflows)

## 7 · Test environments and test data

- [Docker Compose documentation](https://docs.docker.com/compose/)
- [Testcontainers](https://testcontainers.com/)

## 8 · Performance, load and resilience

- [Grafana k6 documentation](https://grafana.com/docs/k6/latest/): thresholds, scenarios, executors
- 📘 *Release It!*, Michael Nygard: timeouts, circuit breakers, bulkheads
- [Principles of Chaos Engineering](https://principlesofchaos.org/)

## 9 · Security, accessibility and compatibility

- [OWASP Top 10](https://owasp.org/www-project-top-ten/) and the [OWASP Cheat Sheet Series](https://cheatsheetseries.owasp.org/)
- [OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications](https://genai.owasp.org/llm-top-10/)
- [WCAG 2.2 at a glance](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/glance/)
- [axe-core rules](https://github.com/dequelabs/axe-core/blob/develop/doc/rule-descriptions.md)

## 10 · Production quality and observability

- [Google SRE Book](https://sre.google/sre-book/table-of-contents/), especially *Service Level Objectives* and *Monitoring Distributed Systems*
- [The Site Reliability Workbook](https://sre.google/workbook/table-of-contents/): implementing SLOs and alerting on burn rate
- [OpenTelemetry documentation](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/)
- [Prometheus: histograms and summaries](https://prometheus.io/docs/practices/histograms/)

## 11 · Test management and quality intelligence

- [DORA research and the four key metrics](https://dora.dev/)
- 📘 *Accelerate*, Forsgren, Humble & Kim

## 12 · AI in QA engineering

- [promptfoo documentation](https://www.promptfoo.dev/docs/intro/): evals, assertions, red teaming
- [Anthropic: define success criteria and build evaluations](https://docs.claude.com/en/docs/test-and-evaluate/develop-tests)
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework)

## 13 · QA platform engineering

- 📘 *Team Topologies*, Matthew Skelton & Manuel Pais
- [CNCF Platforms white paper](https://tag-app-delivery.cncf.io/whitepapers/platforms/)

## 14 · Quality strategy and leadership

- 📘 *Agile Testing* and *More Agile Testing*, Lisa Crispin & Janet Gregory
- 📘 *The Fearless Organization*, Amy Edmondson: psychological safety
- [Google SRE Book: postmortem culture](https://sre.google/sre-book/postmortem-culture/)
