# Paths by role

The topics are designed to be taken in order. If you have less time, or a specific goal, these paths pick the topics that matter most for your role. **Topics 1 and 2 are on every path**, because everything else builds on them.

## QA engineer or SDET: the full path (~75 hours)

All fourteen topics, in order. At three to five hours a week, that's four to six months. Do every lab and at least half of the challenges. Your notebook becomes a portfolio.

## Developer: own the quality of your code (~55 hours)

Focus on the fast layers you own (Topics 3, 4), how your changes reach production safely (6, 10), and the non-functional risks you design for (8, 9). Topic 12 covers testing the AI features you build, as well as using AI to test.

## Lead or manager: decide with evidence (~35 hours)

Read the Concepts of every topic. Do the labs of 1, 2, 6, 11 and 14: they're the ones that turn test results into decisions. Topics 13 and 14 are written for you: scaling quality across teams, and setting strategy, investment and metrics.

## Study with others

The course works well for a study group: one topic every week or two, everyone does the lab alone, then you meet for an hour to compare notebooks and walk through the challenge. The [community page](community.md) has a suggested format.
