## Two days — everything, with room to discuss

**Day 1:** Modules 1-3, Labs 1-4, plus [Lab 5: Performance](labs/lab-05-performance-k6.md).
**Day 2:** Modules 4-8, Labs 6-9, and a team exercise: draft *your* team's quality gate and metrics using Lab 7 and Lab 8 as templates.

## Tracks for a mixed room

| | QA / SDET | Developers | Managers & leads |
|---|---|---|---|
| Must-do labs | 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9 | 2, 4, 6, 7 | 7, 8, 9 |
| Optional labs | 5, 8 | 1, 3, 5 | 3 (as observers), 6 |
| Modules to lead discussion in | 2, 5 | 3, 4 | 1, 6, 7 |

Pair people across tracks for the labs. A manager reading quality-gate output next to the developer who just broke it is the most useful conversation the workshop produces.
